# Datacamp course projects

This folder contains a series of projects that I completed as part of several tracks on Datacamp. All data sets are saved in each project's corresponding folder and was provided by Datacamp for the purpose of students answering real life business questions. 

## Projects

### Exploring market trends 
<b>Purpose:</b> Takes three files of three different file formats and merges them, cleans the data and answers the business questions listed below for the purpose of evaluating market pricing, supply of private rooms and data recency.\
<b>Business questions answered:</b>\
&emsp;1. When were the earliest and most recent reviews dated?\
&emsp;2. How many listings were private rooms?\
&emsp;3. What was the average listed price?\
<b>Libraries:</b> pandas and numpy.

### Hypothesis testing
<b>Purpose:</b> Reads two csv files, selects the portion of the data dated from 2002-01-01, prints a histogram to show both dataframes are left skewed, uses a Wilcoxon-Mann-Whitney test to find the p_value then prints the p-value together with if the test rejects the null hypothesis or fails to reject it. \
<b>Business questions answered:</b>\
&emsp;Null hypothesis (H₀): Women's FIFA World Cup matches do not tend to have higher total scores than men's matches.\
&emsp;Alternative hypothesis (H₁): Women's FIFA World Cup matches tend to have higher total scores than men's matches.\
<b>Libraries:</b> pandas, numpy, matplotlib.pyplot and pingouin. 

### Model and predict insurance claims
<b>Purpose:</b> Identify which customer characteristic is most useful for predicting whether a customer will make an insurance claim or not for the purpose of assessing risk among customer segments and resource allocation.\
<b>Business questions answered:</b> Which customer feature is the most accurate predictor of whether a customer will file a claim or not?\
<b>Libraries:</b> pandas, numpy, statsmodels.formula.api and sklearn.metrics.

### Preparing data for modeling
<b>Purpose:</b> Reads a large dataset, transforms categorical data with two options to boolean, changes datatype to minimize memory usage, segments several columns into categories and uses some of those categories to answer the business question.\
<b>Business questions answered:</b> What does the data look like for candidates with at least 10 years experience who work at companies with 1000 employees or more?\
<b>Libraries:</b> pandas. 

### Visualize crime data
<b>Purpose:</b> Identify patterns in crime data that could help local law enforcement agencies understand when, where and who is most likely to be affected by crime in their city.\
<b>Business questions answered:</b>\
&emsp;1. What time during a 24h period do crimes occur most frequently?\
&emsp;2. Which areas experience the highest volume of crime at night (when night is defined as 10pm - 3am)?\
&emsp;3. Which age group experiences the most recorded crimes? 
<b>Libraries:</b> pandas, numpy, matplotlib.pyplot and seaborn.
